Membership FAQ

Common questions.

Can I switch memberships later?

Yes. Most members start on Baseline and upgrade to Optimize within the first year. You can change tiers at any time, and we'll prorate the difference.

Is the membership refundable?

If you cancel within 30 days of your baseline assessment, we offer a full refund minus the cost of any completed lab work. After that, memberships run until your renewal date.

Does insurance cover membership?

Memberships are private-pay. Some lab work may be reimbursable through HSA or FSA accounts. We'll provide itemized receipts to help you file claims if eligible.

Can I add family members?

Yes. Family bundles are available with a 15% discount per additional member. Contact our team for a custom quote.

What if I move cities?

Most ongoing care can be delivered remotely. If you move to a city without a Longeva clinic, we'll coordinate local lab partners for your retests at no extra cost.
