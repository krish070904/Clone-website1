Head of Nutrition

Dr. Sara Ackerman, RD

Metabolic Endocrinology

Dr. Sara Ackerman specializes in metabolic health, insulin sensitivity, and healthy aging. With more than fourteen years of clinical experience, he helps members understand how nutrition, movement, sleep, and biomarkers work together to improve long-term health.

His approach combines evidence-based medicine with personalized coaching, helping patients make small, sustainable changes that produce meaningful results over time.

About the practitioner

Background & approach.

Dr. Sara Ackerman believes lasting health comes from consistent, evidence-based lifestyle changes rather than quick fixes. By combining advanced metabolic testing with personalized nutrition and practical interventions, he helps members improve energy, optimize metabolic function, and build long-term resilience. His approach focuses on sustainable progress supported by measurable health data.

His philosophy is simple: small improvements, measured consistently, create extraordinary long-term outcomes.

Training & experience

Sara earned his undergraduate degree in Biochemistry from Northwestern University before completing his Doctor of Medicine at the University of Michigan Medical School. He completed his residency in Internal Medicine, followed by a fellowship in Endocrinology, where his research focused on insulin resistance, metabolic health, and healthy aging.

Before joining Longeva, Sara led metabolic wellness programs for executives and individuals seeking personalized preventive care, helping them achieve measurable improvements through science-backed strategies.

Areas of focus

  • Insulin resistance
  • Weight optimization
  • Hormonal health
  • Body composition
  • Precision nutrition

Beyond the clinic

Outside the clinic, Sara enjoys cycling, roasting specialty coffee, and exploring the latest research in nutrition science. He regularly leads educational workshops that simplify complex health topics, empowering people to make informed decisions about their long-term well-being.
