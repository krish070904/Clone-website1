About Longeva

Medicine, practiced the way it should be.

Built by physicians who believe healthcare should add years to your life not just react when you're sick.

Our story

We built the clinic we wanted to work in.

Longeva began in 2022 with a simple question: why is the most advanced medical science of our time so rarely available outside of research labs and elite athletes?

After fifteen years in internal medicine, Dr. Erika Halberg had watched too many patients fall through the gaps of a system designed to manage disease, not extend life. Insurance dictated the timeline. Twelve-minute consults dictated the depth. Generic guidelines dictated the protocol.

She started Longeva to do the opposite. Time with the patient. Depth in the data. Specificity in the protocol. No insurance gatekeeping. No rushed consults. No copy-paste plans.

Three years later, we are a team of physicians, researchers, and clinicians serving over 1,200 members across three clinics. The model works because it puts the patient not the system at the center.

What we believe

Three principles.

01
Data, not dogma.

Every recommendation we make is grounded in peer-reviewed science and your own biomarkers. We don't follow trends. We follow evidence and outcomes

02
Time over throughput.

Ninety-minute sessions. Same-day messaging. Unhurried thinking. The most valuable thing a clinician can give you is real time and we refuse to compress it.

03
Prevention, not prescription.

The goal isn't to treat disease faster. It's to push the timeline back, by years if not decades. Everything we do measures against that mission.
