Questions

Things people ask us.

What is biological age testing?

We're a direct-pay service, but most members submit their Healthio receipts for FSA/HSA reimbursement, and many commercial plans reimburse out-of-network care. We'll provide the documentation you need.

Who prescribes the treatments?

Only board-certified clinicians licensed in your state. Every prescription is based on your intake, lab work, and a real consultation. Not an algorithm.

How is the lab collection done?

We ship an at-home collection kit with everything you need. Most members complete it in under 10 minutes. If a blood draw is required, we'll route you to a Quest or Labcorp location near you at no extra cost.

What if I'm already on birth control or other meds?

That's completely fine. Your clinician will factor your current medications into your plan. We don't require you to stop anything. If something needs to change, your clinician will walk you through it.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. You can pause or cancel your membership from your dashboard, and we'll refund unused treatment if you cancel within 30 days of a new prescription.

How soon will I see results?

Most members report meaningful symptom change between weeks 4 and 8, but hormone rebalancing takes time. We recommend at least 90 days before evaluating your full response, and we include a re-test to measure it objectively.
